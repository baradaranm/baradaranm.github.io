## Analysis of an Electrically Interconnected Suspension System with various electrical network configurations Under Random or Bump Excitations

**Date:** August 17–20, 2025

**Location:** Anaheim, CA

**Event:** IDETC2025 Conference

### About My Presentation

I had the privilege of presenting my research paper, “Analysis of an Electrically Interconnected Suspension With Various Electrical Network Configurations Under Random or Bump Excitations”, at the ASME IDETC-CIE 2025 Conference in Anaheim, California.

In this work, I explored an electrically interconnected suspension system and conducted energy harvesting analysis by optimizing the electrical circuits that interconnect the suspension system. This approach demonstrates how advanced suspension design can both improve ride performance and generate usable energy from road excitations.

It was an incredible experience to share these findings with leading experts in mechanical engineering, gain valuable feedback, and exchange ideas that push the boundaries of energy harvesting for vehicle systems research.

A huge thank you to the organizers, colleagues, and my mentor Dr.Tai who made this journey possible!
