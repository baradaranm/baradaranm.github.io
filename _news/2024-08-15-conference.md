## Analysis of an Electrically Interconnected Half-Car Suspension System With Resistive Loads Under Random Road Excitation

**Date:** August 25–28, 2024

**Location:** Washington DC

**Event:** IDETC2024 Conference

### About My Presentation

I have presented my paper at AMSE’s IDETC-CIE 2024 in Washington DC.

I attended many presentations and learn about the state of the art research topics specifically in the field of multi-body dynamics, vibrations and control.

Moreover, it was a great experience to meet very professional and well known people in my field.
